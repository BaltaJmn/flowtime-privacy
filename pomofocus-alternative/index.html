Pomofocus on your phone: a native Android alternative

Pomofocus is a Pomodoro timer that runs in the browser, and its own site lists desktop downloads for Mac, Windows and Linux, but no Android or iPhone app. FlowTime is built as a phone app, with a notification you can control, a home screen widget and a quick settings tile.

FlowTime fits if

  • You want a timer that lives on your phone: a notification with pause and stop buttons, a home screen widget and a quick settings tile (Android).
  • You want an alert at the end of each phase even with the app closed, because the timer runs on the clock.
  • You want no ads, and a count-up FlowTime mode where the break follows how long you worked.

Pomofocus fits if

  • You want nothing to install: it opens in any desktop or phone browser, with downloads for Mac, Windows and Linux.
  • You plan with a task list that has pomodoro estimates and a finish-time estimate.
  • You want your timer connected to other tools: Todoist and webhooks (Zapier, IFTTT) are listed in its Premium plan.

Where Pomofocus is the better pick

Nothing to install

It works in any desktop or phone browser, and it has desktop downloads for Mac, Windows and Linux. If you work on a computer that is not yours, a website is the easier way in.

Planning around pomodoros

Tasks with pomodoro estimates, a finish-time estimate and templates; Premium adds projects and yearly reports. FlowTime keeps to today's tasks and tags.

Connected to your other tools

Its Premium plan lists a Todoist integration and webhooks for Zapier and IFTTT, for people who want their timer to talk to other tools.

Questions

Is there a Pomofocus app for Android?

Pomofocus's own site lists a website that works in desktop and mobile browsers, plus desktop downloads for Mac, Windows and Linux. As of October 26, 2026 it lists no Android or iPhone app. FlowTime is a native Android app: it keeps the timer running on the clock, shows a notification with pause and stop buttons and alerts you at the end of each phase even with the app closed.

Is Pomofocus free?

Yes, there is a free version: custom focus and break times, alarm and background sounds, a task list, up to 3 templates and reports by day, week and month. Premium is a paid plan billed monthly or yearly that adds projects, yearly reports, CSV download, unlimited templates, Todoist integration, webhooks and "No Ads". We do not quote its price here, so check it with Pomofocus before you pay.

Can I use FlowTime in a browser like Pomofocus?

In a simple way, yes. This site has a free web timer (flowtime.baltajmn.dev/pomodoro/) with 25/5, 50/10 and FlowTime modes. The notification, widget, quick settings tile, tasks, tags, sounds and Coop live in the app.

Can I move my Pomofocus data to FlowTime?

No. FlowTime cannot import data from other apps or sites, including a CSV from Pomofocus, so you would start with an empty history.

FlowTime on your phone

The same Pomodoro and FlowTime modes with your own times, plus a third one, Percentage, and what a browser tab can't do: the timer in a notification and a widget, today's tasks, 12 focus sounds, and your streak, goal and stats.

Coop is a private room where up to 8 people share one timer: anyone can start, pause or skip the break, so you can study together from different places.

Free, with no ads and no account. Only Coop asks you to sign in.
