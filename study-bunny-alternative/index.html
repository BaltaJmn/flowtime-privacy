A Study Bunny alternative with no ads at all

Study Bunny is a study timer with a reward loop of coins, carrots and a bunny, and its own FAQ says it has no Pomodoro mode. FlowTime is a Pomodoro timer with no ads in any version. Here is how the two compare.

Google Play App Store

Checked on October 26, 2026

FlowTime fits if

  • You want a timer with no ads at all, instead of paying to remove them.
  • You want a Pomodoro mode: fixed blocks with your own times, or FlowTime, where the break comes from how long you worked.
  • You want your data to stay on your phone, with no analytics.

Study Bunny fits if

  • You like a reward loop: coins and carrots, a bunny and items to collect.
  • You want flashcards and a study tracker inside the timer app.
  • You want the app in one of its 14 languages.

Where Study Bunny is the better pick

A reward loop that makes studying feel like play

Coins and carrots, a bunny and items to collect. FlowTime's rewards are plainer: a streak, a daily goal and levels.

Study tools around the timer

Flashcards, a study tracker and 15 subject colors, next to a to-do list.

More languages and a large user base

Translated into 14 languages, per its FAQ, with more than 5 million installs on Google Play.

Questions

Does Study Bunny have ads?

The free version does. Its FAQ says the developers run ads to cover hosting costs, that all video ads are opt-in, and that you can get rid of forced ads with a one-time purchase. Google Play marks the app "Contains ads". FlowTime has no ads in any version.

How do I remove ads from Study Bunny?

With a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Its FAQ lists a $14.99 package (250 coins and carrots, no forced ads and offline use) and a $19.99 package (500 coins and carrots, no forced ads, offline use and a study buddy), and the US App Store list shows the same two prices, as of October 26, 2026.

Is Study Bunny a Pomodoro app?

No. Its own FAQ says it has no Pomodoro mode. It offers a break timer mode and a countdown or stopwatch, so it is a study timer rather than a Pomodoro app. FlowTime has Pomodoro, FlowTime and Percentage modes.

Can I move my Study Bunny data to FlowTime?

No. FlowTime cannot import data from other apps, so you would start with an empty history.
