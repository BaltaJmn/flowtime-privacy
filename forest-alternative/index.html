A Forest alternative with no ads and three timer modes

Forest grows a tree while you stay off your phone, and it can block apps. FlowTime is a plainer tool: a Pomodoro timer with three modes, no ads and no app blocking. Here is how the two compare.

Google Play App Store

Checked on October 26, 2026

FlowTime fits if

  • You want a timer with no ads, in a free app where your data stays on your phone.
  • You want modes beyond the fixed block: FlowTime counts up and sets your break from how long you worked, or as a percentage you choose.
  • You want to focus with others without a game around it: a private Coop room for up to 8 people sharing one timer, with no chat.

Forest fits if

  • You want something that blocks the apps that tempt you: Deep Focus is free on every tier.
  • You want your focus to grow something: a forest, friends and a leaderboard, and real trees planted with Trees for the Future.
  • You use browser extensions or an iPad as well as your phone.

Where Forest is the better pick

It blocks apps

Deep Focus blocks apps that are not on your allowlist, and it is free on every tier (a custom allowlist is Plus). FlowTime does not block apps, so if a lock is what you need, Forest does that and we do not.

Real trees

Coins can plant real trees with Trees for the Future, up to 5 per account with Plus. Its site counts more than 2 million so far.

A bigger world around the timer

Friends, a leaderboard, seasonal events, Apple Health sync, browser extensions and more than 10 million installs on Google Play.

Questions

Is Forest app free?

The core is. Its site lists a focus timer (countdown and stopwatch), Deep Focus app blocking, Plant Together, friends and a leaderboard in the free tier, with no credit card. Plus is a subscription, and its site says the price varies by region and platform. On the US App Store list, as of October 26, 2026, Plus shows as $5.99 monthly and $32.49 or $35.99 yearly, each labeled "Early Bird", and the page does not say which is the current price. The one-time Forest Pro is no longer sold.

Does Forest app have ads?

The free tier does. Its FAQ says it shows occasional ads, never during an active focus session, and that they can also earn in-app rewards. Plus is described as ad-free, and Google Play marks the app "Contains ads". FlowTime has no ads in any version.

Can I move my Forest data to FlowTime?

No. FlowTime cannot import data from other apps, Forest included, so you would start with an empty history. FlowTime can back up its own data automatically and to a file.

Does FlowTime block apps like Forest?

No. FlowTime does not block apps. With Pro it can turn on Do Not Disturb while you work, which quiets notifications but does not stop you from opening anything. If you want a lock, Forest's Deep Focus is the better tool.

FlowTime on your phone

The same Pomodoro and FlowTime modes with your own times, plus a third one, Percentage, and what a browser tab can't do: the timer in a notification and a widget, today's tasks, 12 focus sounds, and your streak, goal and stats.

Coop is a private room where up to 8 people share one timer: anyone can start, pause or skip the break, so you can study together from different places.

Free, with no ads and no account. Only Coop asks you to sign in.
