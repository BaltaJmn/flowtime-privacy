# Design System: FlowTime web

Scope: the Operate pages (`pomodoro/`, `es/pomodoro/`, `flowtime-technique/`, `es/tecnica-flowtime/`) and the Persuade pages (`finals-week/`, `exam-season/`, `es/epoca-de-examenes/`). The older root pages (`index.html` privacy, `terms.html`, `delete.html`, `404.html` with the Coop link fallback) are not part of this design system. Source of truth: `ft/ft.css` (all tokens as `:root` custom properties), `ft/timer.js`, markup generated by `tools/build.mjs`, OG cards by `tools/og.mjs`.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Day Drawn as You Work"**

One deep marine ground, one light-blue ring, thin numerals. The timer is a ring around large light digits; under it, or as the page itself, the day is laid out on a time axis: a clock ruler that fills as blocks are completed (Operate, form "day timeline", seed 537caa6a) or a timetable of exam-day blocks that each start the timer in their mode (Persuade, form "exam-day timeline with start actions", seed dd3ce0d9). Focus is a light-blue solid, rest is a green outline or short bar, so work and break read at a glance.

The mood is calm and unhurried: a dark single-theme surface (`color-scheme: dark`), light weights for display type, heavy weights only for labels and emphasis, no gradients, no hero imagery beyond one phone crop. Depth is tonal.

The build diverges from the OWN-WORLD brief in one value: rest is mint green `#8FCFB5`, not the app's marine `#7C99A8`. The build wins.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One theme only: dark marine ground, ink in pale blue-white.
- Thin tabular numerals (weight 300) as the signature; ring stroke 9 on a 200 unit viewBox.
- Two phase colors: focus-blue and rest-green, used for data (ring, blocks, bars), not decoration.
- Pill and circle controls; 44px minimum touch height.
- Hairline dividers (`--line`, ink at 16%) instead of boxes.

## Colors

A monochrome blue family from ground to ink, plus one green reserved for rest.

### Primary
- **Focus Blue** (#8ECAE6): progress ring, focus blocks and bars, filled buttons, main play control, the colon in the numerals, link underlines, focus outline, selection.

### Secondary
- **Rest Green** (#8FCFB5): the ring while in a break, break blocks on the ruler, the hollow bar of a rest row in the timetable. Only ever means "rest".

### Neutral
- **Marine Ground** (#15506A): page background and the favicon/theme tone.
- **Deep Well** (#103F55): inset surfaces: mode switch, day ruler, alternate bands, footer.
- **Raised Card** (#1D5C78): selected mode, round controls, mode chips, keycaps.
- **Ring Track** (#2B6782): the unfilled ring.
- **Mist Ink** (#EAF6FB): primary text and the now-marker.
- **Soft Muted** (#B5D6E5): secondary text, labels, captions, axis ticks.
- **On Focus** (#0F3A4E): text and icons on Focus Blue.
- **Hairline** (rgb(234 246 251 / 0.16)): dividers and ghost-button borders.

### Named Rules
**The Phase Color Rule.** Focus-blue means working, rest-green means resting, everywhere (ring, ruler, timetable, legend). Neither is used for anything else.

**The Tonal Depth Rule.** Surfaces separate by stepping ground, well and card, never by a new hue.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Mulish (self-hosted `ft/mulish.woff2`, weights 200-1000, `font-display: swap`; fallback system sans)
**Body Font:** Mulish
**Label Font:** Mulish at 700

**Character:** One family at contrasting weights: 300 for large display and numerals, 400 for headings, 700 to 800 for labels, buttons and emphasis. Digits are always tabular.

### Hierarchy
- **Numerals** (300, clamp(56px, 19vw, 84px), 1, -0.03em): timer readout; colon in Focus Blue.
- **Display** (300, clamp(34px, 5.4vw, 54px), 1.08, -0.025em): page h1; tool pages use clamp(28px, 4.4vw, 40px).
- **Headline** (400, clamp(26px, 3.4vw, 34px), 1.2, -0.02em): h2 of each band.
- **Title** (700, 19px, 1.3): h3, timetable block titles (rest rows 600 and muted).
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.6): copy, capped at 66ch; lede 19px muted at 40ch.
- **Label** (700, 15-16px, 1.2): buttons, mode switch, phase name. Small text 12-14px muted (ticks, notes, captions, legend), 13px for captions and keys.

### Named Rules
**The Thin Numerals Rule.** Time is set large and light (300) with tabular figures; weight is spent on labels, never on the clock.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Labels are sentence case at natural spacing; the only tracked text is the 14px language switch (0.04em).

## Layout

Single column of bands inside a 1120px wrap with 20px side padding. Tool pages (`.tool`) centre the timer in a 760px column under a centred title. Seasonal pages open with `.season`: copy on the left, a compact timer (dial up to 248px) on the right in a 1fr / 420px grid with a 48px gap; the ruler is hidden there because the timetable plays that role. Reading bands use 72px vertical padding (56px under 820px) separated by a hairline, with `deep` bands on Deep Well. `.cols` is an auto-fit grid (min 260px, gap 36px 48px); `.split` pairs text with a 340px phone crop.

The timetable (`.day`) is a four-column grid: 88px time, 10px bar, flexible text, auto action; rows have 20px padding and hairline borders. Under 640px the action drops under the text and the time column narrows to 64px. Breakpoints in use: 600px (nav scrolls horizontally with a sticky language switch), 640px, 820px, 900px. Spacing steps seen: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 28, 36, 48, 56, 72.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat and tonal. Depth is conveyed by stepping Marine Ground, Deep Well and Raised Card, plus hairlines. Shadows exist in two places only, both soft and diffuse.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Selected mode** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.2), 0 6px 16px -8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`): lifts the chosen tab in the mode switch.
- **Phone crop** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 4px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.2), 0 24px 48px -16px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55)`): the app screenshot in `.split`.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Unless Pictured Rule.** Interface surfaces carry no shadow; only the selected tab and the screenshot are lifted.

## Shapes

Round and soft: pills (24px) for buttons, a 26px pill track around 22px segments for the mode switch, full circles (56px, 72px main) for timer controls, 20px for the ruler panel, 28px for the phone crop, 12px mode chips, 8px for the logo tile, 4px for ruler block tops, 3-5px keycaps and legend swatches. The dial is a circle with round linecaps. Timetable bars are 10px-wide rounded bars (5px radius); a rest row shows a hollow bar with a 2px Rest Green outline. Dividers are 1px hairlines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** pill, 48px tall (44px inside the timetable), 22px side padding (18px in the timetable), weight 700.
- **Fill:** Focus Blue with On Focus text; hover #A8D7EC. Used for the one primary store action.
- **Ghost:** transparent, hairline border, Mist Ink text; hover turns the border Focus Blue.
- **Transitions:** 150ms ease-out on background and border. Trailing arrow glyph is an inline 18px SVG.

### Round controls
- 56px circles on Raised Card (hover #26698A), 72px main control in Focus Blue; icons are inline SVG (play, pause, stop, skip) with a 13px muted caption beneath. Active scales to 0.96; disabled is 40% opacity.

### Mode switch
- Segmented pill on Deep Well, 44px segments, muted labels; the checked segment is Raised Card with ink text and the selected-mode shadow. Radio inputs sit invisibly on top of each label.

### Navigation
- Brand tile and name left, links right: 15px, 600, muted, 44px high, current page in ink; language switch after a hairline. On narrow screens the links scroll horizontally.

### Dial and readout
- SVG ring r=88 in a 200 viewBox, stroke 9, track Ring Track, progress Focus Blue (Rest Green in break, 300ms colour fade, 400ms linear offset). Phase name 15px muted above the numerals, hint 14px muted below.

### Day ruler (Operate signature)
- Deep Well panel, 34px rail with hairline baseline; focus blocks 30px tall Focus Blue, break blocks 14px tall Rest Green, a 2px ink now-marker, tabular 12px hour ticks, legend swatches. Empty state is a centred muted line.

### Study timetable (Persuade signature)
- Rows of time, bar, title with text and a mode chip, and a ghost start button that starts the timer in that mode. Rest rows use the hollow bar and muted title.

### Tables
- `.ranges` and `.vs`: no fills, hairline row borders, 14px muted headers, tabular figures.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** draw time as time: numerals tabular and light, blocks on an axis.
- **Do** take every colour from the `:root` tokens; Focus Blue for work, Rest Green for rest.
- **Do** keep touch targets at 44px or more and keep the 2px Focus Blue focus outline (3px offset).
- **Do** respect reduced motion (all transitions and animations are off under `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- **Do** keep copy blocks at 66ch or less on a Marine Ground or Deep Well band.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce a red or tomato countdown, a light theme, or a second accent hue.
- **Don't** put weight on the clock: no bold numerals.
- **Don't** add shadows, gradients or boxes to ordinary surfaces; separate with tonal steps and hairlines.
- **Don't** add imagery beyond the phone crop, or ad, social or third-party embeds.
