Last updated: 3 November 2026

The short version. FlowTime is a focus timer. Your sessions, tasks, tags, goals and settings are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded to us. There is no account unless you choose Coop (optional, described below), and no analytics of our own. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which receive some data from your device (see "Ads (free version)" below); FlowTime Pro removes them. On iPhone, if you see ads, iOS asks whether FlowTime may track you, and you can say no. Apart from Coop and the ads, the only data that leaves your phone is what is needed to know whether you have bought FlowTime Pro or left a tip.

Who is responsible

FlowTime ("the app") is published by Baltasar Jiménez ("we", "us"), an individual developer based in Spain, who acts as data controller for the limited processing described below.

Contact for anything in this policy: baltajmn@gmail.com. The full details of the publisher are in the legal notice.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in FlowTime is stored locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Your sessions: when each one started and ended, its mode (Pomodoro, FlowTime or Percentage), and the tag and task you chose for it, if any.
  • Your tasks and tags, your daily goal and your streak.
  • Your settings: timer lengths, theme, app icon, sounds and saved sound mixes, reminder time, and whether you have bought FlowTime Pro or left a tip.
  • The session in progress, so that the timer keeps counting when the app is closed.

The home screen widget, the quick settings button on Android and the Live Activity on iPhone read the session in progress from the same storage. Notifications, alerts and the daily reminder are created on your device. The ambient sounds are generated by the app itself; nothing is streamed.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Your phone's own backup

If backup is turned on in your phone, Android includes FlowTime's sessions, tasks, tags and settings in the backup it makes to your Google account, and in a direct transfer to a new phone. The session in progress is left out. That backup belongs to your Google account and is covered by Google's policies. We cannot read it.

On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is turned on, iOS includes FlowTime's data in the backup it makes to your iCloud account, and the same happens with a backup to a computer. That backup belongs to your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's policies. We cannot read it either.

Files you export

FlowTime can save a backup file with your sessions, tasks, tags and settings, and FlowTime Pro can export your sessions as a CSV file. You choose where each file is saved. Once saved, it is outside our control, and if it goes to a cloud folder or another app, that provider's policy applies to it, not this one. Importing a backup file only reads the file you pick.

What leaves your device

Purchases

FlowTime offers FlowTime Pro, as a one-time purchase or as a monthly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it in your store settings, and optional tips. Payments are processed by the store you installed FlowTime from, Google Play or the App Store; we never see or receive your card details, billing address or any other payment information.

To know whether an installation has bought FlowTime Pro or left a tip, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat assigns each installation an anonymous identifier and stores, on our behalf, that identifier, the purchase history associated with it, and technical information such as the app version, the operating system version and the store country. This identifier is never linked to your name or your email address, and nothing about your sessions, tasks or tags is ever sent to RevenueCat. FlowTime does not send the advertising identifier to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

RevenueCat processes data in the United States. Transfers outside the European Economic Area are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Coop: studying together (optional account)

Everything above works without an account. Only Coop, where you study in a private room with other people, asks you to sign in, with Sign in with Apple or Google. If you never open Coop, nothing in this section applies to you.

When you use Coop we store, on our server: the identifier and the email address that Apple or Google give us when you sign in (with Apple you may choose a private relay address), the name you show (new profiles start with an anonymous default name, not the name of your Apple or Google account, and you can change it at any time), your invitation code, your friends and friend requests, the people you block, the room you are in and its timer (when it started, whether it is paused, its durations), and the reports you send. Other people only ever see your name: your friends, people with a friend request open with you, and the people in your room. Your study history, tasks, tags and goals stay on your phone; Coop never sends them. An automatic word filter stops offensive names from being saved.

The server is run by Supabase, Inc. as a processor, in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). The sign-in system is shared with the developer's other apps, Chroma, Quilt and MoodTraker: if you sign in to more than one of them with the same Apple or Google account, it is one sign-in identity (your email address and your Apple or Google ID), but each app keeps its own name, friends and data, and no app can see another's.

A report sends us an email with the names of the two people involved so that we can act within 24 hours; it is delivered by Resend, Inc. as a processor.

So that Coop can tell you when someone sends you a friend request, accepts yours or invites you to their room, also with the app closed, we store a notification token for your phone with your Coop account. It is handed to Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, on Android) or Apple (Apple Push Notification service, on iPhone) to deliver each notification, together with its text, which includes the chosen name of the person who caused it. The token is deleted when you sign out or delete your Coop account. If you turn notifications off for FlowTime, nothing is shown.

You can change your name, block and unblock people, sign out and delete your Coop account at any time from the app (Settings, Coop account, Delete account; also at flowtime.baltajmn.dev/delete.html). Deleting it removes from the server, at once, everything FlowTime keeps about you in Coop: your name, invitation code, friends and friend requests, blocks, room membership, notification tokens, and the reports you made or that were made about you. If you also use Chroma, Quilt or MoodTraker with the same Apple or Google account, your sign-in identity (your email address and your Apple or Google ID) stays while one of those apps still uses it, and is deleted when you delete your account in the last one; each of those apps keeps its own data until you delete it there.

Ads (free version)

The free version of FlowTime shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the Focus screen and at most one full-screen ad a day, after you finish a session. FlowTime Pro removes all ads. If you have Pro, or you were already using FlowTime before ads were added, the app does not show you ads and does not ask you for ad consent (so on iPhone it does not show the tracking prompt either). FlowTime asks Google to show only ads that are suitable for all ages (content rating G).

When ads are shown, Google's ads software on your phone collects and receives the following, to choose, show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud:

  • your IP address, which Google can use to estimate your approximate location;
  • identifiers of your device: on Android, the advertising ID and the app set ID; on iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and otherwise identifiers that are limited to the app or to its developer on your device (see "Tracking permission on iPhone" below);
  • how you interact with the ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the ads software perform, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use and, on iPhone, crash logs and which ads you have seen.

This data is encrypted in transit. FlowTime does not give the ads software your sessions, tasks, tags, goals or settings. Google handles what it receives under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google is the only ad network FlowTime uses.

Consent in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. There, FlowTime shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) when you open the app and does not request ads until you have answered it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Ad privacy options, which appears only where a form or message applies.

Google is contacted when the app starts. For people who see ads, the consent software contacts Google every time the app starts, to find out whether a form applies to you and to keep your choice up to date. Like any request to Google, this reveals your IP address and the app's details to Google, also where no form is shown.

US states. In US states with privacy laws, FlowTime shows Google's US states message, where you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data and of targeted advertising. You can change that choice at any time in Settings, Ad privacy options.

Tracking permission on iPhone. On iPhone, after the consent form (where it applies) and before the first ad, iOS shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, which asks whether FlowTime may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is available to Google's ads software, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not available and you still see ads, without it; Google's software may still use identifiers that are limited to the app or to its developer on your device. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. If you have Pro, or you were using FlowTime before ads were added, you are not asked.

Android advertising ID. Google's ads software reads your advertising ID, which is why FlowTime declares the normal permission "AD_ID". You can reset or delete the ID at any time in your phone's Android settings; apps then get a string of zeros instead of the ID.

Ratings

FlowTime may show the store's own rating dialog, from Google Play or the App Store. Your rating goes to that store, under its own policy; the app is not told what you rated or whether you rated at all.

Nothing else

FlowTime contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK of our own. The other software that collects data is the purchases library (RevenueCat), the connection to Coop if you use it, and Google's ads software in the free version, all described above; the ads software also uses what it collects for its own analytics and fraud prevention. We do not build profiles, we do not use remarketing, and apart from the ads and the services listed in "Who receives your data" below we do not share personal information with anyone. The app needs an internet connection only for purchases, for the ads in the free version and, if you use it, for Coop.

  • identificadores de tu dispositivo: en Android, el ID de publicidad y el ID del conjunto de apps; en el iPhone, el identificador de publicidad (IDFA) solo si permites el seguimiento, y si no, identificadores limitados a la app o a su desarrollador en tu dispositivo (ver "Permiso de seguimiento en el iPhone" más abajo);
  • cómo interactúas con los anuncios y con la app, como aperturas, toques y reproducciones de vídeo;

Estos datos viajan cifrados. FlowTime no le da al software de anuncios tus sesiones, tareas, etiquetas, objetivos ni ajustes. Permiso de seguimiento en el iPhone. En el iPhone, tras el formulario de consentimiento (donde se aplica) y antes del primer anuncio, iOS muestra la petición de transparencia de seguimiento de apps de Apple, que pregunta si FlowTime puede seguirte por las apps y webs de otras empresas. Si eliges "Pedir a la app que no me siga", el identificador no está disponible y sigues viendo anuncios, sin él; el software de Google aún puede usar identificadores limitados a la app o a su desarrollador en tu dispositivo. Puedes cambiar tu elección cuando quieras en Ajustes de iOS, Privacidad y seguridad, Seguimiento. Si tienes Pro, o usabas FlowTime antes de que existieran los anuncios, no se te pregunta.

ID de publicidad de Android. El software de anuncios de Google lee tu ID de publicidad, por eso FlowTime declara el permiso normal "AD_ID". Puedes restablecer o borrar el ID cuando quieras en los ajustes de Android de tu móvil; las apps reciben entonces una cadena de ceros en vez del ID.

Valoraciones

Nada más

FlowTime no incluye ningún SDK de analítica ni de informes de fallos propio. El otro software que recoge datos es la biblioteca de compras (RevenueCat), la conexión a Coop si lo usas y el software de anuncios de Google en la versión gratis, todo descrito arriba; el de anuncios también usa lo que recoge para su propia analítica y para prevenir el fraude. No elaboramos perfiles, no usamos remarketing y, salvo por los anuncios y los servicios de "Quién recibe tus datos" más abajo, no compartimos información personal con nadie. La app necesita conexión a internet solo para las compras, para los anuncios de la versión gratis y, si lo usas, para Coop.

Esta web

Esta política, el temporizador web y las guías están en flowtime.baltajmn.dev. La web no usa cookies y no tiene scripts de analítica ni de publicidad.

La web está alojada en GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) detrás de Cloudflare. Como cualquier web, reciben tu dirección IP y los datos de tu petición (la página, tu navegador y tu idioma) para entregar la página y protegerla de ataques, y guardan registros para eso con sus propias políticas. Nosotros no leemos esos registros; Cloudflare solo nos da totales anónimos de tráfico. La base legal es nuestro interés legítimo en mantener la web en marcha y segura.

El temporizador web guarda su estado y los bloques de hoy en tu navegador (almacenamiento local). No salen nunca de tu navegador, y borrar los datos del sitio en el navegador los elimina.

Las páginas con botones de tienda preguntan a Apple (itunes.apple.com), desde tu navegador, si la app del iPhone está a la venta, lo que le da a Apple tu dirección IP. Los enlaces a las tiendas llevan una etiqueta de campaña (por ejemplo utm_source=flowtime.baltajmn.dev) para que Google Play y la App Store puedan contar las instalaciones que vienen de la web; de eso no recibimos ningún dato personal.

La página que abre un enlace de sala de Coop (flowtime.baltajmn.dev/r/ seguido del código de la sala) funciona sin cuenta. Cuando abres un enlace así, tu navegador pregunta a nuestro servidor de Supabase (Fráncfort) por el estado de esa sala (su fase, su temporizador y cuánta gente hay), y le envía el código de la sala del enlace y tu dirección IP. La página no muestra nombres.

Cuánto tiempo se conservan los datos

Los datos de tu dispositivo se conservan hasta que los borras en la app o la desinstalas. Los registros de compra que guardan RevenueCat y la tienda se conservan el tiempo que esos servicios necesiten para poder restaurar compras y para cumplir sus propias obligaciones legales y fiscales. Los datos que recibe el software de anuncios de Google los conserva Google con sus propias políticas.

Tus derechos según el RGPD

Si estás en el Espacio Económico Europeo, en el Reino Unido o en Suiza, tienes derecho a acceder, rectificar, suprimir, limitar y oponerte al tratamiento de tus datos personales, y derecho a la portabilidad.

La mayoría los puedes ejercer tú: los datos están en tu móvil. Borrar una tarea, o una sesión al editar el historial de un día, la elimina, desinstalar la app lo borra todo y el fichero de copia es la portabilidad. Para los datos de compra que guarda RevenueCat, también para pedir que se borren, escribe a baltajmn@gmail.com con el número de pedido de Google Play o de la App Store: es la única forma de encontrar tus registros, precisamente porque no guardamos nada que te identifique. Tus datos de Coop los ves, corriges (tu nombre) y borras (tu cuenta) en la app. Lo que Google recibe por los anuncios lo trata Google; puedes pararlo comprando Pro, retirando tu consentimiento en Ajustes, Privacidad de los anuncios (EEE, Reino Unido y Suiza), restableciendo el ID de publicidad en Android o desactivando el seguimiento de FlowTime en los ajustes de iOS.

Bases legales: la ejecución de un contrato (entregarte la compra que hiciste, y prestarte Coop cuando eliges usarlo), tu consentimiento para los anuncios de la versión gratis (que se pide con el formulario de consentimiento de Google y, en el iPhone, con la petición de seguimiento de Apple, y se puede retirar cuando quieras en Ajustes, Privacidad de los anuncios) y nuestro interés legítimo en evitar restauraciones fraudulentas, en atender los reportes para que Coop sea seguro y en mantener la web en marcha y segura. También tienes derecho a reclamar ante la autoridad de control. En España, la Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Si resides en California: en los últimos doce meses hemos recogido las categorías "identificadores" (un identificador anónimo de la instalación y, solo si usas Coop, el identificador de tu cuenta, tu correo y el nombre que elegiste) e "información comercial" (tu historial de compras), para entregarte la compra que hiciste y, si lo usas, prestarte Coop.
