A finals week study day

Long blocks in the morning, shorter ones after lunch, recall in the evening. Move the times to fit your exams; keep the order.

  1. 08:30

    Warm up

    Review yesterday's notes and write down today's three goals.

    1 x 25/5
  2. 09:00

    Hardest subject

    Three 50-minute blocks on the exam that worries you most. Phone in another room.

    3 x 50/10
  3. 12:00

    Lunch, away from screens

  4. 13:00

    Practice exams and problems

    Shorter blocks for the afternoon dip: four rounds of 25/5, timed like the real exam.

    4 x 25/5
  5. 15:00

    Walk outside for 20 minutes

  6. 15:30

    Second subject

    FlowTime: work until your focus fades, then take the break it gives you.

    FlowTime
  7. 17:30

    Dinner and a real break

  8. 19:00

    Active recall

    Flashcards, practice questions, explaining it out loud. Three rounds of 25/5.

    3 x 25/5
  9. 20:30

    Plan tomorrow in five minutes, then stop

Make the schedule work

Sleep is part of the plan

Sleep helps your memory hold on to what you studied. A 1 a.m. session usually costs you the next morning.

Test yourself, don't reread

Practice questions and recall stick better than rereading notes, and they show you what you still miss.

Study with someone

A friend on the same timer makes it harder to quit early. That's what Coop is for.
