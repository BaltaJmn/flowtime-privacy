A revision day

Long blocks in the morning, past papers after lunch, recall in the evening. Move the times to fit your exams; keep the order.

Building your revision timetable

Work back from exam dates

Put each exam in the calendar, then give the nearest and the hardest ones the most mornings.

One subject per long block

Switching subjects every hour feels productive, but each switch costs you time to get back in. Keep each morning for one.

Leave one evening off a week

A planned night off is easier to keep than an unplanned collapse.
