A simpler alternative to Focus To-Do, with the timer first

Focus To-Do joins a Pomodoro timer to a full task manager on almost every device. FlowTime does less on purpose: a timer with three modes, today's tasks and tags. Here is how the two compare.

Google Play App Store

Checked on October 26, 2026

FlowTime fits if

  • You want the timer first and a task list that stays small: today's tasks and tags, not projects and sub-tasks.
  • You want a count-up FlowTime mode, a Percentage mode or a private Coop room, none of which its store descriptions mention.
  • You want a small payment: Pro is $1.99 once, and all three modes, the widget and Coop are free.

Focus To-Do fits if

  • You plan with projects, sub-tasks, recurring tasks, reminders and priorities.
  • You use the same timer on a Mac, a Windows PC, a tablet or a watch.
  • You want a Gantt chart of your focus time and trend charts.

Questions

How much is Focus To-Do Premium?

Focus To-Do is a free download with in-app purchases. As of October 26, 2026, the US App Store list shows Lifetime $11.99, 3 Months $3.99 and an item named "Focus To-Do Premium" at $1.99. The list does not say what each one unlocks. Google Play shows in-app items from $1.99 to $11.99.

Is there a free alternative to Focus To-Do?

FlowTime is free: all three modes, the notification timer, the widget, the quick settings tile, up to 15 pending tasks, 4 tags, all 12 sounds with 3 saved mixes, streak, daily goal, stats for day, week and month, backups and Coop. Pro is optional: $1.99 once, or a monthly plan.

Does FlowTime have projects and sub-tasks?

No. FlowTime works from today's tasks: unfinished ones carry over and you pick a task for each session. Tags group your work, and Pro removes the limit on tags and tasks. If you plan with projects, Focus To-Do fits better.

Can I move my Focus To-Do tasks to FlowTime?

No. FlowTime cannot import data from other apps, so you would start with an empty list and history.

FlowTime on your phone

The same Pomodoro and FlowTime modes with your own times, plus a third one, Percentage, and what a browser tab can't do: the timer in a notification and a widget, today's tasks, 12 focus sounds, and your streak, goal and stats.

Coop is a private room where up to 8 people share one timer: anyone can start, pause or skip the break, so you can study together from different places.

Free, with no ads and no account. Only Coop asks you to sign in.
