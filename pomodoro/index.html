How to use this Pomodoro timer

Pick a rhythm

25/5 is the classic Pomodoro: 25 minutes of focus, then a 5 minute break. 50/10 suits deep work, reading or problem sets, where 25 minutes ends just as you get going.

Press start and keep the tab

The timer runs on the clock, so it stays right in a background tab. A soft chime plays when focus ends and again when the break is over.

Take a real break

Stand up, look away from the screen, drink some water. When the second chime plays, press start for the next block.

Which one to use

25/5

When starting is the hard part, when you are tired, or for review and small tasks.

50/10

For work that needs a warm-up: writing, coding, a chapter, a problem set.

FlowTime

When you don't want a bell to cut you off. Work until you stop; the break fits what you did.

The FlowTime mode

FlowTime counts up instead of down. Work until you reach a natural stop, then press Start break: the break depends on how long you worked.
