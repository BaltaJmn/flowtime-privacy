A Focus Friend alternative for people who want a plain timer

Focus Friend, by Hank Green and Honey B Games, is a cozy focus app with a room to decorate, and it can lock distracting apps. FlowTime is a plain Pomodoro timer. Both are ad-free; the difference is in what you get and what you pay.

Google Play App Store

Checked on October 26, 2026

FlowTime fits if

  • You want a plain timer with three modes: Pomodoro, FlowTime (counts up) and Percentage.
  • You want to focus with others: a private Coop room for up to 8 people sharing one timer, with no chat.
  • You would rather pay once: Pro is $1.99 once, and there is a monthly plan if you prefer it.

Focus Friend fits if

  • You want something that locks distracting apps: Deep Focus Mode has an allow list and is available on Android.
  • You like a cozy room to decorate on your breaks, with hundreds of decorations.
  • You like its art by independent artists and the option of background music.

Where Focus Friend is the better pick

Real app blocking

Deep Focus Mode locks distracting apps and has an allow list, and it is available on Android. FlowTime does not block apps.

A room to make your own

Hundreds of room decorations and art by independent artists, per its FAQ. FlowTime is a plain timer on purpose.

A known name

It is made by Hank Green and Honey B Games, and it has more than 1 million installs on Google Play.

Questions

Does Focus Friend have a subscription?

Yes, it has Focus Friend Pro, and the app is a free download. As of October 26, 2026, the US App Store list shows Pro Monthly $3.99, Pro Yearly $19.99 and Lifetime $39.99, plus a lifetime offer and a welcome offer at $29.99 each. FlowTime Pro is $1.99 once, or a monthly plan, and adds advanced stats, CSV export, unlimited tags, tasks and mixes, 6 more sounds, 6 themes, 6 icons and automatic Do Not Disturb while you work.

Does Focus Friend have ads?

No. Its FAQ says the subscription exists so the app can be ad-free and privacy-first, and Google Play does not mark it "Contains ads". FlowTime has no ads either, so ads are not a reason to pick one over the other.

Does Focus Friend block apps?

Yes. Deep Focus Mode locks distracting apps and has an allow list, and it is available on Android. FlowTime does not block apps.

Can I move my Focus Friend data to FlowTime?

No. FlowTime cannot import data from other apps, so you would start with an empty history.

FlowTime on your phone

The same Pomodoro and FlowTime modes with your own times, plus a third one, Percentage, and what a browser tab can't do: the timer in a notification and a widget, today's tasks, 12 focus sounds, and your streak, goal and stats.

Coop is a private room where up to 8 people share one timer: anyone can start, pause or skip the break, so you can study together from different places.

Free, with no ads and no account. Only Coop asks you to sign in.
