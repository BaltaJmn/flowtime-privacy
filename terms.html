The short version. FlowTime is free to use, with ads that Pro removes. Pro is optional and can be bought two ways, with the same result: once, for good, or month to month. Nothing you created is ever deleted if Pro ends. Coop, the optional way to study in a room with friends, has zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive users: every report is reviewed within 24 hours, and names or accounts that break these terms are removed. If a name or an account is removed, you get an email with the reason and you can appeal by replying to it.

These terms apply to the two optional parts of FlowTime: Coop, which needs an account (below), and Pro, which is paid. Without an account, FlowTime keeps your sessions, tasks and settings on your phone and shares them with no one. The free version shows ads served by Google; what Google receives for that is in the privacy policy.

FlowTime is published by an individual developer based in Spain. The details are in the legal notice.

Coop: studying together

You must be 16 or older to use Coop. By continuing to sign in you confirm that you are, and you accept these terms. In Coop other people see your chosen name, your friends and the people in your room. Choose a name you would be happy for a friend to read.

What is not allowed

This applies to your visible name and to anything you do to other people in Coop (invitations, friend requests, rooms). Any of these gets the name changed or the account deleted, depending on how serious it is:

  • Nudity, sexual content or violence in a name. Any content that sexualizes minors is also reported to the authorities.
  • Threats, harassment or hate against anyone for their origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other trait.
  • Bullying, intimidation, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Illegal content, content that breaks someone else's rights, or other people's private information.
  • Spam, advertising, or sending friend requests and invitations to people who do not want them.

Reporting, blocking and removing

Open the menu (the three dots) next to a person in your room or in your friends:

  • Report name reaches us at once. We act within 24 hours: we change the name or delete the account of whoever broke these terms. Nobody is told who reported.
  • Block ends the friendship; that person stops seeing you and cannot share a room with you or invite you again. They are not told. You can unblock from Settings, Coop account.
  • Remove from room: the person who created the room can remove someone from it.
  • Leave room and Remove friend end your part in a room or a friendship without telling the other person.

You can delete your Coop account at any time from Settings, which erases everything FlowTime keeps about you in Coop. If you use other apps by the same developer with the same Apple or Google account, the privacy policy explains what stays.

When a name or an account is removed

If we change your name or delete your account because it breaks these terms, we send you an email, to the address of your sign-in. It says what we did, which rule was broken and why, and how to appeal. We send it when we do it, or as soon as we can, unless the law forbids us to tell you (for example during an investigation by the authorities).

To appeal, reply to that email. A person reads every appeal and answers it. If we were wrong, we undo what we did: a name is put back; a deleted account cannot be recovered, but you can create a new one. Every decision on a report is taken by a person, not automatically. An automatic word filter only stops offensive names from being saved; if it blocks a name that is fine, write to us and a person looks at it.

Reportar, bloquear y quitar

Abre el menú (los tres puntos) junto a una persona de tu sala o de tus amigos:

Puedes borrar tu cuenta de Coop cuando quieras desde Ajustes, y eso borra todo lo que FlowTime guarda de ti en Coop. Si usas otras apps del mismo desarrollador con la misma cuenta de Apple o Google, la política de privacidad explica qué se queda.

Si cambiamos tu nombre o borramos tu cuenta porque incumple estos términos, te enviamos un correo, a la dirección con la que entraste. Lo enviamos en el momento o en cuanto podamos, salvo que la ley nos prohíba avisarte (por ejemplo durante una investigación de las autoridades).

Para recurrir, responde a ese correo. Una persona lee cada recurso y lo contesta. Si nos equivocamos, deshacemos lo que hicimos: un nombre se devuelve; una cuenta borrada no se puede recuperar, pero puedes crear otra. Cada decisión sobre un reporte la toma una persona, no un sistema automático. Un filtro automático de palabras solo impide guardar nombres ofensivos; si bloquea uno que está bien, escríbenos y lo mira una persona.
