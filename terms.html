FlowTime Terms of Use

Last updated: 27 October 2026

English - Español - Privacy policy

The short version. FlowTime is free to use. Pro is optional and can be bought two ways, with the same result: once, for good, or month to month. Nothing you created is ever deleted if Pro ends. Coop, the optional way to study in a room with friends, has zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive users: every report is reviewed within 24 hours, and names or accounts that break these terms are removed.

These terms apply to the two optional parts of FlowTime: Coop, which needs an account (below), and Pro, which is paid. FlowTime without an account keeps everything on your phone and shares nothing with anyone.

Coop: studying together

You must be 16 or older to use Coop. By continuing to sign in you confirm that you are, and you accept these terms. In Coop other people see your chosen name, your friends and the people in your room. Choose a name you would be happy for a friend to read.

What is not allowed

This applies to your visible name and to anything you do to other people in Coop (invitations, friend requests, rooms). Any of these gets the name changed or the account deleted, depending on how serious it is:

  • Nudity, sexual content or violence in a name. Any content that sexualizes minors is also reported to the authorities.
  • Threats, harassment or hate against anyone for their origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other trait.
  • Bullying, intimidation, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Illegal content, content that breaks someone else's rights, or other people's private information.
  • Spam, advertising, or sending friend requests and invitations to people who do not want them.

Reporting, blocking and removing

Open the menu (the three dots) next to a person in your room or in your friends:

  • Report name reaches us at once. We act within 24 hours: we change the name or delete the account of whoever broke these terms. Nobody is told who reported.
  • Block ends the friendship; that person stops seeing you and cannot share a room with you or invite you again. They are not told. You can unblock from Settings, Coop account.
  • Remove from room: the person who created the room can remove someone from it.
  • Leave room and Remove friend end your part in a room or a friendship without telling the other person.

You can delete your Coop account at any time from Settings, which erases everything of yours on the server. We may delete an account that breaks these terms without notice.

Pro, two ways to buy

PlanPriceWhat it is
ForeverShown in the app before you buy (1.99 EUR in Spain)One payment. It does not renew and it does not expire.
MonthlyShown in the app before you buy (0.99 EUR per month in Spain)An auto-renewing subscription that renews every month until you cancel.

Both unlock the same Pro features. Prices in other countries are set by the store and include taxes.

Renewal and payment

The monthly plan renews automatically each month at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple Account or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase and at each renewal. The store, not FlowTime, handles payment and has your payment details.

How to cancel

  • iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > FlowTime > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play Store > profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > FlowTime > Cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date so it does not renew. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid. Cancelling does not refund the current period; refunds are decided by the store under its own policy.

When Pro ends

Pro closes again, and FlowTime tells you so. FlowTime never deletes your sessions and settings: what is above the free limits stays there, visible, until you buy Pro again. You can restore a purchase at any time from the Pro screen on the same store account.

Coop: estudiar juntos

Hay que tener 16 años o más para usar Coop. Al seguir adelante para entrar confirmas que los tienes y aceptas estos términos. En Coop otras personas ven el nombre que elijas, tus amigos y a la gente de tu sala. Elige un nombre que te parezca bien que lea un amigo.

Lo que no se permite

Vale para tu nombre visible y para lo que hagas a otras personas en Coop (invitaciones, solicitudes de amistad, salas). Cualquiera de estas cosas supone cambiar el nombre o borrar la cuenta, según lo grave que sea:

  • Desnudos, contenido sexual o violencia en un nombre. Cualquier contenido que sexualice a menores se denuncia además ante las autoridades.
  • Amenazas, acoso u odio contra cualquiera por su origen, religión, género, orientación sexual, discapacidad o cualquier otro rasgo.
  • Intimidar, hostigar o hacerse pasar por otra persona.
  • Contenido ilegal, que vulnere derechos de otros, o información privada de otras personas.
  • Spam, publicidad, o mandar solicitudes de amistad e invitaciones a quien no las quiere.

Reportar, bloquear y quitar

Abre el menú (los tres puntos) junto a una persona de tu sala o de tus amigos:

  • Reportar el nombre nos llega al momento. Actuamos en menos de 24 horas: cambiamos el nombre o borramos la cuenta de quien incumplió estos términos. Nadie sabe quién reportó.
  • Bloquear termina la amistad; esa persona deja de verte y no puede compartir sala contigo ni invitarte otra vez. No se le avisa. Puedes desbloquear en Ajustes, Cuenta de Coop.
  • Sacar de la sala: quien creó la sala puede sacar de ella a alguien.
  • Salir de la sala y Quitar de amigos terminan tu parte en una sala o una amistad sin avisar a la otra persona.

Puedes borrar tu cuenta de Coop cuando quieras desde Ajustes, y eso borra todo lo tuyo del servidor. Podemos borrar sin aviso una cuenta que incumpla estos términos.

Renovación y pago

El plan mensual se renueva automáticamente cada mes al precio que viste al suscribirte, hasta que lo canceles. El pago se carga a tu cuenta de Apple o de Google Play al confirmar la compra y en cada renovación. Quien gestiona el pago y tiene tus datos de pago es la tienda, no FlowTime.

Cómo cancelar

  • iPhone: Ajustes > tu nombre > Suscripciones > FlowTime > Cancelar suscripción.
  • Android: Google Play Store > icono de perfil > Pagos y suscripciones > Suscripciones > FlowTime > Cancelar suscripción.

Cancela al menos 24 horas antes de la fecha de renovación para que no se renueve. Conservas Pro hasta el final del periodo ya pagado. Cancelar no devuelve el periodo en curso; los reembolsos los decide la tienda con su propia política.

Cuando Pro termina

Pro se cierra otra vez y FlowTime te lo dice. FlowTime nunca borra tus sesiones y ajustes: lo que está por encima de los límites gratis se queda ahí, visible, hasta que vuelvas a comprar Pro. Puedes restaurar una compra cuando quieras desde la pantalla de Pro, con la misma cuenta de la tienda.
